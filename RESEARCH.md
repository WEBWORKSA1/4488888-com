## 2. Chosen idea (most usable / profitable)

**4488888 — Lucky Numbers Lab**: a multi-culture lucky-number analyzer + numerology calculators + premium-number market intelligence, with a dedicated **valuation & brokerage lead desk** for phone numbers, plates, numeric domains and addresses.

Why this beats alternatives (pure numerology blog, lottery site, parked domain):
- **Traffic:** evergreen searches (number meanings, angel numbers, life path, lucky numbers, "is 4 unlucky", plate records) + shareable tool results.
- **RPM:** content sits next to high-CPC verticals — domains, telecom, autos, luxury, real estate, finance.
- **Lead value:** a single brokered numeric domain/plate/number deal can exceed a year of AdSense.
- **AdSense-safe:** no lottery/gambling predictions; educational framing; full legal pages.

Revenue stack: AdSense Auto ads → valuation/broker leads (commission) → sponsorships (tool, newsletter, contest) → affiliates (marketplaces) → donations → paid reports (phase 5).
