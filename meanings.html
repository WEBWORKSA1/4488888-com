Famous number phrases

Chinese number-speak reads digits as near-homophones. These codes appear in prices, phone numbers, plates and wedding dates.

168

yī lù fā — prosper all the way

Common in shop prices and business numbers.

518

wǒ yào fā — I will prosper

Popular sale price and lottery-style pick.

520

wǒ ài nǐ (approx.) — I love you

May 20 is an unofficial romance day; love-themed prices.

1314

yī shēng yī shì — for a whole lifetime

Here 4 is read as 世 — a positive four. Often paired as 5201314.

666

liù liù liù — everything smooth

Gaming slang for "awesome"; very different from the Western reading.

888

fā fā fā — triple prosperity

The default luxury suffix for hotels, plates and domains.

28

yi faat (Cantonese) — easy prosperity

Hong Kong plate "28" sold for about US$2.1M.

99

jiǔ jiǔ — long, long time

Weddings and anniversaries.

14

yào sǐ — avoid

Often skipped in floors and plates.

24 / 74

"easy death" / "surely die" (Cantonese) — avoid

Discounted in Hong Kong markets.

49

shi-ku (Japanese) — avoid

Rarely used for hospital rooms.

13

Western — avoid

Missing 13th floors; triskaidekaphobia.

Case study: reading 4488888

The number behind this site is a neat illustration of how one sequence reads differently across markets.

  • Pattern: seven digits, only two distinct, with a run of five identical eights. Pattern-wise that is near the top of any memorability scale.
  • Chinese: the 88888 ending is prestigious — a mobile number with that tail sold for ¥2.25 million at a Beijing court auction in 2020. The double-4 prefix is the obvious objection; the positive counter-reading treats each 4 as 世 (generation), the same move that makes 1314 a love number.
  • Western numerology: 4+4+8×5 = 48 → 12 → 3 when fully reduced; read as 4 (foundation) leading into 8 (wealth), it is a "build first, then harvest" sequence.
  • Vedic: root 3 is ruled by Jupiter — expansion and good counsel.

Takeaway: the same number can be premium or problematic depending on the audience. Score it for your market, not for a generic one.
