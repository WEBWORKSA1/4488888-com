About the Lab

Independent · reader-supported · est. 2026

4488888 Lucky Numbers Lab is an independent publication and toolset about one surprisingly powerful idea: people attach meaning to numbers, and that meaning moves behaviour and money.

A licence plate sells for the price of a skyscraper floor. A phone number ending in five eights draws thousands of auction bids. Apartment towers skip their fourth floors. Retailers end prices in 8 for one audience and 9 for another. We document these patterns carefully, across Chinese, Japanese, Indian and Western traditions, and turn them into tools anyone can use.

What we do

  • Free tools — the Lucky Number Analyzer, numerology calculators, an auspicious date finder and more.
  • Research — sourced guides on number culture and on the markets for plates, phone numbers and numeric domains.
  • Valuation desk — free, confidential valuations and buyer matching for owners of premium numbers.

Our editorial standards

  • We cite sources for prices and historical claims, and update figures when better data appears.
  • We describe beliefs as beliefs. Numerology and number symbolism are cultural traditions, not science, and we say so.
  • Sponsored content is always labelled. Advertisers never influence tool results.
  • We correct errors quickly — report one here.

How we're funded

Display advertising, optional brokerage services, sponsorships and reader support. The tools stay free.

The name

"4488888" is a numeric domain name — four-four followed by five eights. We chose it because it captures the whole subject in seven digits: a number that one culture reads as foundation-then-fortune and another reads with caution. The same digits, many stories.
