Methods in plain language

Life Path

Reduce the year, month and day separately to a single digit (or master number 11, 22, 33), then add and reduce again. Reducing components first avoids the common error of summing all eight digits at once, which can hide a master number.

Pythagorean vs Chaldean

Pythagorean numerology assigns 1–9 to letters in alphabetical order (A=1 … I=9, J=1 …). Chaldean numerology, older and sound-based, uses only 1–8 — 9 is treated as sacred and is not assigned to any letter — and pays attention to the unreduced "compound" total. The two methods often disagree; that disagreement is itself useful when testing brand names.

Personal Year

Add your birth month and birth day to the year in question and reduce. The nine-year cycle runs from 1 (beginnings) to 9 (completion).

Auspicious dates

Our finder ranks calendar dates by digit root, by the presence of 8 and absence of 4, and by your occasion. Traditional Chinese almanacs (Tong Shu) and Indian Panchang calculations rely on lunar positions; use them for ceremonies where tradition matters.

These tools describe traditions. They are not predictions, and they should not drive medical, legal or financial decisions.
