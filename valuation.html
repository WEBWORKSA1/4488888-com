FAQ

Before you submit

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. We earn money from advertising, optional brokerage and partner referrals — never from charging you for an opinion.

Will my number be published?

No. Requests are handled privately. Nothing you submit appears on this site without your written permission.

Do you guarantee a sale price?

No one honestly can. We give a range based on comparable sales, cultural fit and liquidity, and we tell you which market is most likely to pay it.

Can you help me buy a specific pattern?

Yes — choose "Find me a number", describe the pattern (e.g. ending 8888) and your budget. We search registries, marketplaces and private holders.
