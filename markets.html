Price drivers

What makes one number worth more than another

📏 Length

Fewer characters = rarer. There are only 10 one-digit, 100 two-digit and 1,000 three-digit .com names — supply is fixed forever.

🔁 Repetition

Runs (8888), mirrors (6886) and sequences (12345) raise recall. Advertisers pay for numbers people remember after one hearing.

🧧 Cultural fit

8, 6, 9 lift value in Chinese-speaking markets; 4 can cut it sharply. Japanese buyers avoid 4 and 9. Western buyers mostly ignore both.

🏛️ Jurisdiction

Plates can only be used where issued. Gulf and Hong Kong markets are deep; most US states cap vanity value through format rules.

🔄 Transferability

Domains transfer globally in days. Phone numbers port only within a country and carrier rules. Some plates require a qualifying vehicle.

⏱️ Timing

Numeric-domain prices peaked in 2015–16 and corrected. Lunar New Year and charity auctions draw the highest bids for plates.
