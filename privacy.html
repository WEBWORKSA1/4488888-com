Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information 4488888.com ("we", "us") collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (valuation, contact, newsletter, donation pledge, contest entry or job application) we receive the details you enter — typically your name, email address and message, and any optional fields such as phone number or country. We use this only to respond to you and to deliver what you asked for.

2. Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit). That service processes the submission on our behalf to forward it by email. We do not sell form data.

3. Calculator inputs

Numbers, names and dates you type into our tools are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our servers unless you choose to submit them in a form.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

5. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

6. Local storage

We store small preferences in your browser (theme choice, cookie-notice choice, tool counters). These never leave your device.

7. Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, generally no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires otherwise.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or India's DPDP Act) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data. Send a request through our contact form with the topic "Privacy request".

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
